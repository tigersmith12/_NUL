What is—and is not—emergent in SUBSTRATE/0.2?

The honest boundary

NUL is a non-LLM artificial-life simulation. It uses a seeded random generator, a continuous 4-channel reaction-diffusion/wave medium, causal delta traces, an internal critic gate, and optional symbolic compression. It does not call an AI service, load a model, or use training data.

Zero pre-defined objects or verbs

  • No objects or food pellets: The world is solely a 32×20×4 numerical grid Φ(x,y,t) governed by a seed-derived conservative skew-symmetric coupling matrix and a sorted fast-to-plastic timescale spectrum.
  • Agent-defined boundaries: Coherent contours on the canvas (loci) exist because the agent segmented local gradients into regions of interest.
  • Self-crystallized operators: At the hardware level the agent only outputs continuous velocity and 4-channel field coupling. Repeatable gestures that produce reliable physical deltas crystallize into reusable motor operators (μ01, μ02, …).
  • Habitat sculpting beyond energy: Slow plastic channels retain concentrated imprints, letting the agent carve calm shelters, waveguides, and persistent anchors that refract waves and sustain homeostatic viability without consuming disappearing items.

Meaning with and without an ontology

Every 8-tick cycle forms a self-selected PurposeSeed, deliberates across candidate traces/operators through an internal Critic, executes on the medium, and evaluates predicted_delta vs. actual_delta. Switch between Emergent (starts ontology-free and coins symbols only under trace compression pressure), None (strictly ontology-free forever), and Imported (tests transplanted beliefs against a new seed’s physics and falsifies broken ones).

Canvas lenses

Toggle Combined, Agent (pure black void except what the agent itself has sensed, segmented, or built), and Medium (raw observer 4-channel field) at any time without affecting the simulation.

This is an experiment in world-definition, causal agency, and representation—not evidence of consciousness or general intelligence. Export the ontology or snapshot to inspect exact numerical state.
